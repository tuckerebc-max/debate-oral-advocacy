# Construct and source ledger

**Registry:** `EXP-DEBATE` / `COMP-DEBATE-ADVOCACY`  
**Design status:** `READY_WITH_CONDITIONS`  
**Project snapshot:** 2026-08-11

## Construct boundary

The competency is comparative advocacy under an explicit format contract. It includes motion, burdens, case, evidence, warrants, speech sequence, clash, rebuttal, weighing, procedure, and adjudication. It is not a universal speaking scale, public-deliberation model, mediation process, or legal-practice standard.

## Release conditions

Pin each format edition, distinguish written rules from community judging norms, use original motions/cases, and preserve accessibility for pace, AAC, alternative modes, and interruption conditions. Transfer to writing, listening, and public argument requires separate evidence.
